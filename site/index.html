Current research & open source

My current research asks how to evaluate agents over sequences of decisions. Building and operating agent systems exposed failures in memory, evidence use, consistency, and coordination. These experiments examine specific parts of that problem: whether decisions track changing conditions, and whether explanations track the evidence behind them.

CoherenceBench Exploratory evaluation

A controlled experiment in whether a model keeps tracking changing conditions over a long session. The model monitors six subsystems across 200 sequential decisions. I compare what it discusses with the action it chooses as the source of the problem changes.

FACET Exploratory evaluation

An experiment on attribution faithfulness in decisions involving multiple factors. Four probes examine which factors a model says matter, how it assigns importance, and whether those explanations respond to changes in the evidence. Earlier working paper on SSRN.

These are personal experiments, not validated benchmarks or peer-reviewed research.
