I go by Venkat Reddy. Based in Bengaluru, India.

Email / GitHub / LinkedIn

I’m an independent AI researcher and systems engineer. My work has taken me from real-time control software to industrial optimization, language models, and agent systems. Today I’m investigating autonomous scientific discovery: AI systems that can question a problem’s formulation, try different methods, design experiments, and revise their approach when the evidence changes. I’m exploring this through Dexter’s Laboratory, FrontierOR, and other experiments.

At Bosch, I developed production engine-control software in embedded C for Infineon TriCore and RTOS platforms, including a HINO software-sharing implementation. At Caterpillar, I worked on software releases, integration, and hardware-in-the-loop validation for C175 and 3500 power-generation systems. At Continental, I applied Bayesian optimization to power-liftgate calibration, reducing a roughly three-week engineering process to about 1.5 hours. I also worked on reinforcement learning for fuel and emissions optimization, supplier selection, industrial data systems, and forecasting.

At Samsung Research, I worked on Bixby anomaly detection, request relevance, and NLP systems connecting user feedback with production telemetry. I led root-cause analysis work combining language models, log parsing, and anomaly detection, and co-invented a published patent application in this area. At Sigmoid, I developed contextual reasoning, query planning, SQL generation, and evaluation for Data Companion, alongside multi-agent reasoning over retrieval and dynamic knowledge graphs.

As founder of PranaAlpha Labs, I built PortoAI, a multi-agent investment research system used by real users, and Lokaah, AI-native search and knowledge infrastructure based on entity resolution, typed relationships, and provenance. The difficulties of building and operating these systems inform my current research into how agents use evidence, coordinate work, and change direction. I’m also interested in physical AI and robotics, drawing on my earlier work in embedded and control systems.
