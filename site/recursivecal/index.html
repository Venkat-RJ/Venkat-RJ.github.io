MODEL NOTES

What this workbench simulates

A rigid tailgate hinge with two powered struts, driven by an illustrative controller. The animated mechanism uses the same mount geometry as the physics model.

Assembly
69 CAD components with articulated hatch and struts
Cycle
0° to 78° over 4.8 s, then hold to 6.5 s
Time resolution
10 ms integration; trace sampled every third step, plus the final frame
Controller presets
Hand-set values informed by the injected synthetic fault
Scope
Browser model demonstration. No ECU, HIL hardware, OEM measurements, autonomous diagnosis, or trained RL controller.

CSV exports contain both full-cycle trajectories. JSON exports also include plant parameters, controller settings, metrics, and the playback position.
